## Scope

Whole product, one pass: landing, pricing and tools (Persuade); workspace, files, recipes, account, metrics (Operate). Shared tokens and components.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard at Linear, Vercel, Notion and Stripe craft. The file and its changes are the only loud things; it refuses gradient headlines, glass, glow and filler icon cards.

OWN-WORLD: Cool zinc neutrals on white and true near-black; one violet from the mark, only for primary actions, focus and what just changed. Geist Sans for everything, Geist Mono for data and SQL. 8px controls, 12px panels, hairline borders with soft two-layer shadows.

STORY: A visitor watches a messy sheet become clean, learns the recipe replays next month with no AI, trusts the privacy default, and starts free.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left: 60px tight headline in two weights, a two-line subhead, Start free and See how it works. Right: a live workspace frame, steps rail, grid mid-fix, change bar. Hairline nav above.

FORM: category standard (canon), chosen after two rounds; seed 49630106.
